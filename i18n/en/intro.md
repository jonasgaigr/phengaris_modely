The Dusky Large Blue (*Phengaris nausithous*) and the Scarce Large Blue (*Phengaris teleius*) are butterflies of wet and mesic meadows with great burnet (*Sanguisorba officinalis*). Females lay their eggs in its flower heads; the caterpillars first feed inside the flowers and then complete their development in the nests of *Myrmica* host ants. Both species therefore depend on meadow management that lets great burnet flower during the adults' flight period (July–August).

Both butterflies are listed in Annexes II and IV of the Habitats Directive (92/43/EEC), so Sites of Community Importance (SCIs) of the Natura 2000 network are designated for them. The Directive and the Czech list of qualifying features still use the older genus name *Maculinea*. In Czechia, the Dusky Large Blue is a qualifying feature of `r n_sites[["nausithous"]]` SCIs and the Scarce Large Blue of `r n_sites[["teleius"]]` SCIs; `r n_both` SCIs are designated for both species.

This page shows how much of these SCIs is covered by the preferred habitat of each species according to a model of potential distribution. The model layer assigns a probability of occurrence to polygons of the Consolidated Layer of Ecosystems of the Czech Republic (KVES). We treat polygons with a probability of at least `r p_min_lab` as preferred habitat, in all KVES categories. Each species is assessed only in the sites where it is a qualifying feature.
